PDFs, without the wait.

k-pdf is a fast, lightweight PDF viewer and editor. Read, edit text, highlight, fill in and create forms, sign, scan, redact and compress, in an app that opens in a fraction of a second.

Everything you do with PDFs, in one small app

Each feature has a step-by-step guide. Click one to see how it works.

Read comfortably

Smooth scrolling, thumbnails, sections, clickable links, and it remembers where you stopped.

Find words, even in scans

Search the whole document, including scanned pages and photos, with built-in text recognition.

Edit text

Click any paragraph and type. The text keeps its font, size and color.

Highlight and comment

Four highlight colors and notes, saved as standard PDF comments.

Fill in and create forms

Fill in any form, or turn a paper form into one people can fill in.

Sign

Draw your signature, type it in a handwriting font, or take it from a photo.

Scan with your phone

Turn photos of documents into clean, flat, scanned pages in one PDF.

Clean up handwritten notes

Faint pencil and blotchy scans become crisp and easy to read.

Redact for good

Black out text and images. What's under the box is removed from the file.

Arrange pages

Reorder, insert, delete, rotate and crop pages.

Present and print

Full-screen slides with a presenter view, and printing with a preview of every sheet.

Dark pages for reading

Light text on soft gray, with diagrams still recognizable and photos untouched.
